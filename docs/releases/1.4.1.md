Assembly Studio 1.4.1

## Fixed

- Switching between light and dark no longer leaves the window's minimise / maximise / close buttons behind. They
  were Windows' own and changed colour a beat after the rest of the window. They are now the program's own, drawn
  in the title bar, and change together with everything else. Close turns red under the pointer, as before. (Windows
  11's snap-layout menu on the maximise button is not available on them; Win+Z still opens it.)
- No more yellow outline on buttons. The browser engine drew a focus ring in the system's accent colour (on Windows
  often yellow) on a button the program focused by itself: the default button of a question, or the tutorial's
  buttons. It is no longer drawn.
