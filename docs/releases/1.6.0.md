Assembly Studio 1.6.0

## Changed

- **Status bar and Assemble panel, redesigned.**
  - **Status bar:** a flat strip along the window's bottom edge. The state comes first, then each fact after a
    thin rule, the keys as small key caps at the far end, and the language and theme switches at a readable size.
  - **Assemble panel:** its result is one line of words ("Assembled · 30 instructions · Saved · just now")
    instead of a row of boxes.
- **Status words are English in both languages,** like the panel and button names: the whole status bar and the
  Assemble panel's state. Assembler errors, their hints and what to do next are still in Korean or English.
- **Inspector:**
  - **Explanations:** every instruction is explained the same way each time. The fields are named, with this
    instruction's register or number, e.g. "rt(`$a0`)에 저장된 값을 shamt(`2`)만큼 왼쪽으로 옮겨 rd(`$v0`)에 넣습니다".
    A register's current value is no longer part of the sentence; the Registers panel shows it.
  - **Field table:** the table under the bits is gone. The bit boxes already show each field's name and meaning.
  - **Colours:** the fields are in the theme's greys instead of blue, green, amber and purple.
- **Registers:** a changed Hex value is no longer drawn with a glow, just in the changed colour like Dec and Bin.
- **Assembler hints:** they now ask you to check the name ("명령어를 확인해 주세요: `syscalll`") instead of saying
  "there is no …".

## Fixed

- The tutorial's card no longer flickers:
  - it no longer fades in again when the Skip button appears;
  - it no longer jumps to the middle of the window while what it points at is being drawn;
  - it no longer twitches by a few pixels.
- No focus ring on the language and theme switches.
