Assembly Studio 1.3.2

## Fixed

- Editor: `Enter` keeps the indentation of the line it is pressed on, so an indented block goes on indented. A line
  left with nothing but indentation keeps no trailing spaces.
- Editor: `Ctrl+/` on an empty line puts `# ` there (after its indentation) and the cursor after it, ready to write
  a comment. Before, it did nothing on an empty line.
