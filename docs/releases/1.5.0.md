Assembly Studio 1.5.0

## New

- **A new tutorial**, rebuilt from the start for both ISAs, in Korean and English: 8 chapters, 31 steps, covering
  everything on the work screen. You do the things yourself where it matters:
  1. A look around: the toolbar, the Editor.
  2. Assembling: Assemble and its result, the Text tab, pseudo-instructions, the register groups.
  3. One line forward, one back: Step, what changed, **Step back**.
  4. Registers and the Inspector: Hex / Dec / Bin, pinning and naming a register, inspecting an instruction and
     its bits.
  5. Memory: the Data tab, a store, the stack.
  6. Controlling a run: breakpoints, Run, Reset, running at 1 line/s.
  7. Input, output and errors: typing a number into the Console, what Step back cannot undo, assembler errors and
     how to fix them.
  8. Writing your own: the toolbar icons, editing keys, Settings, the panel splitters, the language and theme
     switches.

  The example program now reads a number from the Console and adds it to the result.

## Fixed

- Shift+F10 is no longer taken for F10 during the tutorial.
